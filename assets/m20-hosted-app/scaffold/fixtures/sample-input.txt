Weekly status, engineering, week 30.

The ingestion pipeline moved to cursor-based pagination on Tuesday after
the vendor deprecated offset queries. Nightly runs now finish in 14
minutes, down from 41. Two incidents: the rate limiter fired during the
Wednesday backfill (resolved by honoring Retry-After instead of konami
retrying), and the staging database filled its disk Thursday because the
old snapshots were never rotated. Snapshot rotation is now a cron job with
a seven-day window.

Open items: the report formatter still drops rows with missing dates
silently, which contradicts the contract; fix is scoped for next week.
Hiring: two systems candidates onsite Friday.
